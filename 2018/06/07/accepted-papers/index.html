We are announcing the list of accepted to AIST 2018 papers – see them below grouped by track.
  1. 107 papers have been submitted totally (including abstracts only submissions). 29 papers successfully passed through the review process and were accepted for presentation at AIST 2018 and publication in Springer’s in Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series.
  2. Apart from mentioned above as an encouragement for young researchers and ongoing studies we invite some of the papers to be published in CEUR-WS or discussed at poster session with no further publication.

Looking forward to meet you on AIST, and please remember to fill in the field “Do you need an invitation” in the registration form, in case you need an invitation for visa application or for you company/university.
